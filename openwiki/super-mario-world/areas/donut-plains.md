# Área: Donut Plains

Segunda área do caminho principal. Fases **Donut Plains 1 a 4**, as casas fantasma (**Donut Ghost House**, **Donut Secret House**), a **Donut Secret 1** e o **Ludwig's Castle**. Derrotar Ludwig libera a **Forest of Illusion**.

## Gating

- **Donut Plains 2** → secret leva ao **Green Switch Palace** (blocos "!" verdes).
- **Donut Plains 1** → secret leva à **Donut Secret 1** (que leva à Donut Secret House).
- **Donut Secret House** → secret leva à **Star World 1** — a porta da Star Road.
- **Ludwig's Castle** → boss Ludwig libera a Forest of Illusion.
